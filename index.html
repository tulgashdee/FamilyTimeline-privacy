Family Timeline is an app for keeping a family's history together: a shared timeline of memories and a family tree. It is made by an independent developer. This policy explains, in plain words, what the app stores, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What the app stores

  • What you and your family add. People in the family tree (names, gender, dates of birth and death, a few words about them, a profile photo, and who is whose parent or partner) and memories (a title, the story, a date, photos, the kind of moment, and who it is about). Each photo is kept at the size it was added (at most 1600 pixels) together with a small preview for the timeline and the tree.
  • A sign-in for each phone. The first time the app goes online, the phone gets an anonymous account. There is no email, phone number or password. This account records which families the phone belongs to, which person in the tree it is, and when it joined.
  • The family's code, the 8 letters and numbers that relatives type to join, and any one-time codes a member makes (each works once, for 7 days).
  • Joining requests. If a family's owner asks to let newcomers in first, the name you type when asking to join is shown to the owner until they let you in or decline.
  • Wrong codes. When a phone types a code that doesn't work, the time is noted against its sign-in, so that nobody can guess codes: after 10 wrong codes in an hour, the phone has to wait. These notes are deleted after a day.
  • Crash reports. When something goes wrong in the app, it sends what went wrong (the error and where in the app's code it happened), the app version and the phone's system (for example "Android 16"). Crash reports contain nothing you or your family added.
  • Settings on your phone, such as the language you chose. These stay on the phone.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, advertising ID or browsing, and it does not track you. It shows no ads.

Why

Everything above is used only to run the app: to keep your family's history, show it to your family's members and keep their phones in step. Crash reports are used only to find and fix problems. Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or shared for anyone else's purposes.

Where it is kept

  • Online with our hosting provider, Supabase, on servers in Seoul, South Korea. Connections are always encrypted (HTTPS), and the data is encrypted where it is stored.
  • On the phones of the family's members, so the app works without internet. Photos are downloaded to a phone when they are first shown there.
  • Like most apps, the app's data on your phone may be included in your phone's own backup to your Google account. You can turn this off in your phone's settings.

Who can see it

  • Only the members of a family can see that family: its people, memories and photos. This is enforced by the database's security rules, not just by the app.
  • The person who started the family (its owner) can see the list of phones that joined, which person in the tree each one is, and when it joined, and can remove anyone who shouldn't be there.
  • Anyone who has the family's code can join the family, so share the code only with your family. The owner can make a new code at any time; the old one then stops working.
  • When you share a memory or the family tree as a picture, it goes wherever you choose to send it.
  • Supabase stores the data on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes. No one else receives it.

Children

Family trees often include children, added by their parents or relatives. The app is not directed at children under 13. A parent can delete anything about their child at any time (see below), or ask us to.

How long it is kept

What your family adds is kept until it is deleted as described below. Crash reports are kept only as long as they are needed to fix problems.

Deleting your data

  • A memory or a person: delete it in the app (on the memory, or on the person's page: ⋮ → Delete from family). It is deleted online too: what it said is cleared and its photos are removed.
  • Leave a family: tap the family's name → Leave this family. If you were the last member, the family is deleted with all its memories and photos.
  • Delete your account: tap the family's name → Delete my account. You leave every family and your sign-in is deleted. What you shared stays with families that still have other members, as part of their history, and any member can delete it there. Families where you were the only member are deleted completely.
  • Without the app: email tuvshintulga21@gmail.com with the family's name and your name, and we will delete your data within 30 days.

Your rights

You can see and correct what your family stores at any time in the app. You can also ask us, by email, for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, and we will answer within 30 days.

Security

All connections are encrypted, access to each family is checked by the database for every request, and there are no passwords to steal. No system is perfectly secure, but we take care to keep your family's history safe.
