Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Delete your data

Family Timeline is an app for keeping a family's history together: a shared timeline of memories and a family tree. This policy explains what the app stores, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What the app stores

  • What you and your family add: people in the family tree (names, gender, dates of birth and death, short biographies, profile photos, who is whose parent or partner) and memories (titles, stories, dates, photos, and who shared them).
  • A sign-in for each device: the first time the app goes online, the device gets an anonymous account. There is no email, phone number or password. This account records which families the device belongs to and which person in the tree it is.
  • Invite codes you create to invite relatives.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, advertising ID or analytics, and it shows no ads.

Where it is kept

  • Online with our hosting provider, Supabase, on servers in Seoul, South Korea. All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • A full copy of each family is kept on the phones and computers of that family's members, so the app works without internet.

Who can see it

Only members of a family can see that family. This is enforced by the database's security rules, not just by the app. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else. Supabase stores it on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes.

Children

Family trees often include children, added by their parents or relatives. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

  • Leave a family: tap the family's name → Leave this family. If you were the last member, the family is deleted with all its memories and photos.
  • Delete your account: on your profile, ⋮ → Delete my account. You leave every family and your sign-in is deleted. What you shared stays with families that still have other members, as part of their history; any member can delete memories and people in the app. Families where you were the only member are deleted completely.
  • Without the app: email tuvshintulga21@gmail.com with the family's name and your name, and we will delete your data within 30 days.

Data is kept until it is deleted as above.
